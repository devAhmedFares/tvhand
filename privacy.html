Privacy Policy — TVHand

Last updated: 2026‑11‑04

This policy explains what data the TVHand app (Android package com.fares.tvhand) and the free TVHand TV helper app handle, and why.

Who we are

TVHand is made by Fares Apps, an individual developer based in Egypt (the developer named on TVHand's Google Play page). Fares Apps is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: remotysupport@gmail.com.

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Controlling your TV happens directly between your phone and your TV on your home network. We never see it.
  • The only personal data we receive is an email you choose to send us.

Data that stays on your phone and TV

To find and control your TV, the app looks for TVs on your Wi‑Fi network and connects to them directly. It keeps on your phone the TVs you connected (name, brand, model and local network address), the pairing codes or keys the TV gives it, and your settings. Button presses, typed text, mouse and keyboard input go only from your phone to your TV (over Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth or infrared) and are never sent to us. The TV helper app, if you install it, only receives commands from your phone on the same network. You can delete all of this by removing a TV in the app, clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Free trial

The 3‑day free trial needs no payment details. To stop the trial from restarting after a reinstall, the app saves the date the trial started in its own storage and in Google Block Store on your phone (a Google Play services feature that keeps a small piece of app data on the device and may carry it to a new phone when you transfer your data). This date is not sent to us.

Purchases

TVHand Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. Google processes your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see them. The app asks Google Play only whether your Google account has an active TVHand subscription, to unlock Pro features. Google Play may also show us sales reports without your payment details.

Files the app downloads from our website

The app downloads small files from our website (devahmedfares.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages): a settings file that can switch features on or off, the latest version number of the TV helper app, and the helper app itself when you install or update it. As with any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information when these files are downloaded, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.

When you contact us

"Report a problem" and "Report your TV" open your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. Besides your text, it contains technical details that help us fix the problem: app version, phone model, Android version, app language, subscription state (trial, Pro or free), your TV's type, model and setup details, and the local network addresses of your phone and TV (addresses inside your home network, such as 192.168.x.x, which cannot be used to find you on the internet). Nothing is sent unless you press Send. When you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and what you wrote.

We use these emails only to answer you and to fix and improve compatibility with TVs, and, where your message is about your subscription, to help you with it. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which stores it for us. We do not share your emails with anyone else unless the law requires it. We keep them for as long as needed to solve your issue and then for up to 2 years, after which we delete them. You can ask us to delete them earlier.

Crash reports

If you allowed it in your phone's settings, Google Play may give us crash and performance reports (for example phone model, Android version and where the app failed). They do not include your name or contact details. We use them only to fix bugs.

Permissions

  • Internet, Wi‑Fi and network state, Wi‑Fi multicast: to find TVs on your Wi‑Fi network, connect to them, and download the files described above.
  • Bluetooth (connect, advertise): only if you use the Bluetooth remote, so your phone can pair with the TV as a keyboard and mouse.
  • Infrared: only on phones with an IR blaster, to send remote signals to the TV.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the remote in the notification shade.
  • Vibration: optional feedback when you press a button.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Where data is processed

We work from Egypt. Our email provider (Google) and website host (GitHub) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own privacy policies and security safeguards. By writing to us, you understand that your email is handled this way.

Security

Downloads from our website use HTTPS. Data kept on your phone is protected by Android's app storage. Only Fares Apps can read the support mailbox.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal data we have about you, to give you a copy, to correct it or to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent or object to how we use it. Write to remotysupport@gmail.com; we answer within one month and do not charge for it. Because we hold no account data, we may ask you to write from the same email address you used before. If we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why, and you can ask us to look at it again by replying.

You also keep the rights given by the data protection law of your country, such as Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, and you can complain to your local data protection authority (in Egypt, the Personal Data Protection Center). We do not sell or share your personal information, including as "sell" and "share" are defined in California law, we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and we treat you the same whether or not you use these rights.

Giving us data is always optional: you only need to email us if you want help. We do not make automated decisions about you.

Children

TVHand is a general‑audience app and is not directed at children. The Family feature is a tool for parents, used from the parent's phone, and does not collect data about children. We do not knowingly receive data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country); if a child has emailed us, ask us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data (for example by adding an online feature), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
