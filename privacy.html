Privacy Policy — TVHand

Last updated: 2026‑11‑04

TVHand is a TV remote‑control app made by Fares Apps, Egypt. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Contact: remotysupport@gmail.com.

We don't collect personal data

TVHand has no account, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

What stays on your network

TVHand talks to your TV directly over your home Wi‑Fi. Button presses, what you type and what you watch go only from your phone to your TV and are never sent to us.

Files the app downloads from our website

The app reads small files from our website (devahmedfares.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages): a settings file that can switch features on or off, the version number of our free TV helper app, and the helper app itself when you install or update it on your TV. Like any website, GitHub receives your phone's IP address when these files are downloaded (see GitHub's privacy statement). We don't receive or keep any of this.

Purchases

TVHand Pro purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled by Google Play (or the App Store). We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to payment data.

The 3‑day free trial is tracked only on your phone: the app saves the date the trial started in its own storage and in Google's on‑device Block Store, so reinstalling the app doesn't restart it. This date is never sent to us.

When you contact us

"Report a problem" and "Report your TV" open your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. Besides your text, it contains technical details to help us fix the problem: the app version, phone model, Android version, language, subscription state (trial, Pro or free), the type and model of your TV, how it is set up, and the local network addresses of your phone and TV (addresses inside your home network, like 192.168.x.x). Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use these emails only to answer you and fix problems, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Permissions

  • Internet, Wi‑Fi and local network — to find and control the TV on your network.
  • Bluetooth — only if you use the Bluetooth remote: your phone pairs with the TV as a keyboard and mouse.
  • Infrared — only on phones with an IR blaster, to send remote signals to the TV.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the remote in the notification shade.
  • Vibration — optional feedback when you press a button.

Crash reports

If you opted in on your device, Google Play / the App Store may share anonymous crash and performance statistics with us.

Your rights

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we may hold is an email you sent us; we process it to answer you (our legitimate interest in providing support). Write to the contact address above and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

TVHand is not directed at children under 13. The Family feature is operated by a parent from the parent's phone.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example an AI assistant), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
