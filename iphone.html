TVHand for iPhone

A smart TV remote on your iPhone, over your home Wi‑Fi: arrows, volume, keyboard and apps. It works with Samsung, LG, Google TV, Android TV and Roku TVs; some features depend on the TV.

If the TV is not found

  • Put the iPhone and the TV on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  • Turn the TV on with its own remote, then open TVHand again.
  • When the iPhone asks to find devices on your local network, choose Allow. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • When the TV shows a code or an Allow message, accept it on the TV.

Subscription

TVHand Pro is an auto‑renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through your Apple Account. New subscribers start with a 3‑day trial; after it the subscription renews at the price shown in the app unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Manage or cancel it on your iPhone in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple.
