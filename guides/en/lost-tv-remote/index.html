Lost your TV remote? Your phone is enough

Lost the remote or the batteries died? Your phone can become the TV remote in a minute, as long as the TV is on Wi-Fi.

Get TVHand on Google Play

Power and the infrared remote are free. Everything else is in Pro, with a 3-day free trial, no card.

Steps

  1. Get TVHand from Google Play.
  2. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi the TV uses.
  3. Open the app and it looks for the TV by itself. Choose your TV.
  4. If your phone has an infrared (IR) blaster, you can control even non-smart TVs, for free.

If it doesn't work

TV not on Wi-Fi? Use the infrared remote if your phone has one.
