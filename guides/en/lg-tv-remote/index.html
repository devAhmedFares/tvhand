LG TV remote from your phone

TVHand works with LG webOS smart TVs, including a real mouse you move with your finger.

Get TVHand on Google Play

Power and the infrared remote are free. Everything else is in Pro, with a 3-day free trial, no card.

Steps

  1. Turn on the TV and keep your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open TVHand and choose your LG TV in the list.
  3. Choose Allow on the TV when it asks.
  4. Use the remote, the mouse or typing from your phone.

If it doesn't work

No popup or no connection? Turn on “LG Connect Apps” or “Mobile TV On” in the TV settings and try again.
Pressed Deny? Connect again from the app and the popup comes back.
